# Independent CPU and MODFLOW 6 terrain validation — 16 October 2026

This programme supplies independent nonlinear solves and physical comparisons. It **does not certify the terrain model**. The original slow-convergence example has additional accepted solution branches beyond the two previously compared GPU trajectories.

1. **Did independent CPU nonlinear solves converge?** Yes for all three priority cases, using both sparse Newton/direct-Picard and frozen/direct Picard from their original cold heads. Newton/direct-Picard also converged for the 1e-2, 1e-3 and 16 controls. The 96 control failed cold: pure Newton failed line search; the completed bounded hybrid reference stopped for nonlinear stagnation at 253 outers, physical RMS 0.0637377 m³/day. A longer interrupted attempt is not counted as a completed success.
2. **Which GPU solutions were confirmed?** At n32 low-K, both cold CPU methods reproduce the GPU Newton branch. At n64 low-K and ordinary K, cold CPU methods select additional branches. Independent CPU solves from perturbed GPU states resolve and confirm both original GPU roots at approximately roundoff-level residuals. This verification-only initialization is separate from cold success. The 16 and 96 GPU roots agree after independent polishing; the latter is not cold CPU convergence.
3. **Did MF6 converge and conserve?** All seven custom-contact TPFA models did. The closest custom-contact XT3D model did in six cases; n32 low-K failed from both tested initial guesses and with a separate stronger-effort SIMPLE-relaxation attempt. Final reported native fluxes were independently decoded, sources checked and cell balances reconstructed. TPFA also has independently re-evaluated nonlinear flux/residual checks; an independent fixed-head XT3D nonlinear evaluator remains unavailable. Native convergence plus a checked reported-flow balance is not a full model-validation certificate.
4. **How faithfully does MF6 represent the target?** Inputs, source ownership, units, indexing, conductivity axes and sampled geometric centres/volumes are verified. The operators differ in geometry, horizontal averaging/saturation, floor treatment and native contact laws. The custom coupling retains the original contact algebra at indexed MF6 heads, with its geometric interpretation explicitly limited. Neither native DRN nor that coupled experiment is claimed equation-equivalent to DarcyWarp.
5. **What causes material discrepancies?** Strong numerical evidence supports distinct roots of the existing discrete equations, rather than insufficient final convergence or a CPU/GPU residual implementation mismatch. A flat analytic control proves this law can have three roots, including two smoothly wet roots with inactive floor. Geometry/discretisation and constitutive/boundary mismatches explain why MF6 is not a same-equation parity test. The n32 closest-MF6 failure is linked experimentally to its connection-elevation setting; its precise derivative/globalization mechanism remains unresolved.
6. **Do they matter physically?** Yes for local head, wet-layer selection and interlayer-flow distribution. The original n32 branches differ by 1.57 m in head and up to 1.89 m in the existing water-table diagnostic. Accepted closest-MF6 results differ by metres and hundreds of m³/day in geological-unit transfer. Bulk net coastal discharge can remain nearly identical because recharge is fixed and all states conserve; that agreement cannot select a branch.
7. **Which production changes are justified?** Three focused control/diagnostic defects are fixed with package regressions: fictitious steady storage constraint, missing remaining-budget Picard refinement in Newton fallback, and rejected rows counted as accepted Newton steps. Their reruns retain the original stopping iterations and solution branches; measured late Krylov differences are disclosed below. Newton stays experimental and opt-in; no K, floor, storage, geometry, source or final acceptance change is promoted. Earlier affine-accuracy wording is qualified, without changing conductances.
8. **What remains unresolved?** Physical branch selection/stability, full-case continuum/refinement uncertainty, the closest n32 MF6 nonlinear failure, cold CPU n96 convergence, and fully independent nonlinear XT3D cross-evaluation. Specific next experiments are below. A converged numerical branch or a published harness is not certification of groundwater predictions.

## Different accepted states: evidence and cause

Every returned head is evaluated without advancing it through the same independent DarcyWarp operator/budget. CPU Newton has an independently derived analytic sparse Jacobian, including coefficient derivatives and full gradient/metric support. Smooth directional differences across several step sizes reach relative errors around 1e-9–1e-10; kink tests use selected one-sided branches. Frozen sparse residuals/backward errors are checked. The CPU process can solve with Warp imports prohibited.

The following are numerical clusters at a predeclared 1e-4 m investigation scale, **not a theorem enumerating all roots**. Both GPU roots and each materially different cold CPU root are independently polished, perturbed deterministically and cross-started in GPU methods. Verification-only restarts are never passed off as cold baselines.

{{BRANCHES}}

For full cases, constrained Jacobians factor without a missing-datum nullspace. Principal-row-scaled condition estimates are typically O(1e4–1e5); direct remaining corrections near polished roots are tiny compared with centimetre/metre branch gaps. Nonzero positive off-diagonal entries show that the full operator is not an M-matrix argument for uniqueness. Extended-precision flux arithmetic, using the stored FP64 geometry, retains small residuals. These checks make stopping-width and gross linear-error explanations inadequate for the material polished differences. They do not prove a continuum uniqueness theorem.

The original n32 pair has maximum difference at (k,j,i)=(0,24,16); ordinary-K GPU branches at (0,53,26). The additional primary CPU Newton branch differs most at (0,58,19), and CPU Picard at (0,52,20). Indices are zero-based, northward j. `root_evidence.json` records physical coordinates, head-minus-bottom, active sets, per-layer norms, precision checks, conditioning and every pair's flux differences. A smaller reduced boundary problem retains only a recorded square of free heads, freezes all other heads to a specified polished field, and retains the original full geometry/gradient stencils. No boundary cut is hidden. Some first reductions fail to retain a second converged state; those failures remain labelled. Larger reductions retain distinct roots. The compact 196-unknown fixture independently resolves two perturbed roots separated by about .377 m in a normal CPU regression.

**A concrete mechanism, not just small residuals:** the distinct flat scalar control has one free unknown and positive source .8 m³/day. Its smooth wet equation is `4h/(h+2)*(h-2)+(h+1)=.8`, giving `5h²-5.8h+.4=0`. Roots .0736404439531 and 1.086359556047 m are both smoothly above the .01 m floor. The constant-floor branch adds -.157073170732 m. Independent CPU and GPU Newton reproduce all three; the middle constrained derivative is negative. This proves that the retained harmonic saturation/constant-vertical law can be non-monotone and nonunique on a declared control without sloping geometry or floor switching at the two wet roots. MF6's different upstream law predicts and measures h=1.266666666667 m. No value was fitted to GPU heads. This does not establish which full-case branch is physically meaningful, or classify physical stability.

Direct CPU Picard was also run on the reduced discrepancy cases; some branches converge and others stagnate under that iteration. Exact factorization does not guarantee that a Picard direction descends the nonlinear residual or that its basin matches Newton's. These are solver/branch observations, not votes about physical correctness.

## MF6: faithful inputs, different equations

Native convertible-cell DRN rejects the original 2 m outlet stage below the owning topsoil bottom. That rejected original deck is preserved. A native three-confined-contact-cell control is explicitly altered physics; its apparent success is not reproduction of the original problem. The custom CONTACT WEL feedback keeps original source Q separate and evaluates original contact algebra independently at MF6 heads. Interior equations still differ, and the saturated-centroid geometry limits the contact's physical mapping.

Source and live checks establish: Cartesian K is unrotated; WEL SOURCE allocation matches every cell exactly at FP64 text precision; no RCH reassignment, automatic source reduction, storage or transient warmup occurs. FloPy's default text precision initially perturbed sources by about 5e-10 m³/day per cell; those allocations failed the source check and were rerun at 17 digits. Binary destination package identity is `paknam2`; diagonal FLOW-JA residual entries are excluded from physical fluxes. Original raw inputs, stdout/listings, outer/inner CSVs, heads, DATA-SAT, CBC and GRB files are preserved losslessly.

The independent TPFA reevaluation at fixed heads matches final native fluxes to approximately 1e-9 m³/day and yields relative cell residuals near 1e-10. Native XT3D reported-flow balances are independently reconstructed; its full fixed-head nonlinear residual has not been independently re-evaluated. This limitation is an explicit matrix dimension, not a passing test.

## Geometry and constitutive error budget

The progressive controls include constant h, h=z, nonzero fully saturated affine fields, flat anisotropic/layered series flows, varying geometry, wet states, floor-active states and discharge gates. Existing analytical/scientific regressions cover h=z exterior fluxes, natural/overridden contacts, conservation and connected components. The new live MF6 analytic tests check head, directional face flow, layer resistance, row orientation and exact source allocation; native success flags alone cannot pass.

The final 320 m-square refinement holds physical lateral planes, analytic geology and top/bottom boundary surfaces fixed; projected integrated contact geometry is unchanged. CPU/GPU natural terrain contacts and MF6 physical-Kz GHB on its stepped surface give the same h=z physical target. XT3D head errors are about 1e-13–6e-13 m. TPFA errors .07035/.09104/.10663 m do not approach that target across n=8/16/32, and it carries spurious lateral flows despite conservation. Per-face flux shrinking with cell width is not convergence of flux density. An earlier moving-fixed-cell-centre ladder remains a labelled control, not the fixed-boundary result. The original 16/32/64/96 family remains a fixed-dx extent ladder.

![Fixed-physical-domain geometry control](../validation_artifacts/terrain_2026-10-16/fixed_domain_refinement.png)

A separate nonzero fully saturated affine control clarifies the older “affine exactness” wording. DarcyWarp uses harmonic half-cell thickness horizontally; a linearly reconstructed physical midpoint face has arithmetic thickness. Vertical metric cancellation remains exact, but the horizontal physical face flux is an approximation when thickness varies. Its normalised error quarters on a common face under refinement; CPU/GPU head errors on the affine control decrease 1.744e-6→5.092e-7→1.280e-7 m. MF6's corresponding error stays around 1.46e-4 m on that horizontal ladder at fixed vertical discretisation. These are geometry/discretisation findings, not unresolved nonlinear iteration residuals or permission to replace conductances.

Same-head term-removal experiments separately quantify geometry, floor and their interaction, remaining upstream-versus-harmonic differences, and native-versus-natural outlet laws. Interactions are nonzero; their magnitudes cannot simply be added as an error budget. Wet/dry/contact full-solve controls and a distinct .001 m-floor control are preserved. None substitutes for the original .01 m problem or constitutes independent unsaturated-flow validation.

## Remaining scientific work

- **Full-case branch certification/selection:** interval/active-set root analysis on the supplied 196-unknown and larger reductions, followed by a justified continuum comparison. If stability is investigated, first declare and justify physical Sy/Ss and boundary evolution, then compare perturbation transients; pseudo-transient numerical convergence alone is insufficient.
- **n32 MF6 XT3D failure:** at its saved late state, expose a no-update native residual evaluation through the MF6 API and compare directional differences with the assembled Newton matrix, especially saturated-centroid connection geometry. Retain heads/operators before and after to prove no nonlinear update occurred. Current source inspection plus angle control is suggestive, not that verification.
- **Original physical-domain refinement:** declare how the original fixed coastal *cells* represent a physical reservoir/plane, construct common continuous geology/source density and fixed integrated outlet patches, and refine horizontally and vertically without moving those physical constraints. Separate harmonic-area, saturated-centroid, vertical-metric and floor/contact effects in a controlled factorial comparison. Restore the exact target equations at any continuation endpoint. The present analytical geometry refinement is not a full unconfined original-case continuum-error estimate.
- **n96 independent cold reference:** inspect its saved stagnating field and compare direct Newton/Picard directions against independently verified derivatives/active sets; test a declared globalization change without altering the physical problem. Warm root confirmation already exists and must not be relabelled cold success.

Until those questions are resolved, water-table/discharge predictions from the terrain model require the stated branch/formulation uncertainty. The evidence justifies the focused implementation fixes and a reusable validation infrastructure. It does not justify choosing whichever branch resembles MF6, declaring all accepted solutions interchangeable, or promoting the experimental Newton path.
